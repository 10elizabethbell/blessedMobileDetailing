# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single file: index.html with inline CSS, no frameworks, no tracking, system font stack (pinned by user).

## Users

Ocean County, NJ car owners (homeowners, commuters, office workers) who want their car detailed without driving to a shop. They mostly arrive on a phone, often from Facebook, and want to know what's offered and how to book in seconds.

## Product Purpose

Blessed Mobile Detailing is a mobile auto detailer that comes to the customer's home, office, or anywhere in Ocean County. This one-pager is a free demo sample pitched to the owner; success means the owner wants it, and visitors text to book.

## Positioning

They come to you: driveway or office lot, no drop-off, no waiting room. Booking is a text message.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: Full Detail (Interior + Exterior), Interior Detail, Exterior Detail, Express Refresh.
- Phone/text: (929) 795-8823. Primary action is SMS with a pre-filled body.
- Service area: Toms River, Brick, Seaside Heights, Lacey, Manahawkin, and surrounding Ocean County towns.
- Prices unknown: show "Text for a quote", never invented prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Existing logo (from their Facebook page): black ground, metallic gold crown over a script "B", silver car silhouette, "BLESSED / CAR DETAILING". Source: logo-source.jpg. User chose to match this black-and-gold world over the original blue brief.
- Footer must carry "Demo one-pager — free sample."

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo only. No photos, reviews, testimonials, years in business, hours, or owner name are confirmed. Do not fabricate any. Facebook photos were not retrievable without login; to be added later if the user supplies them.

## Product Principles

1. Text-to-book is the whole funnel; it's always one thumb away.
2. Say only what's true; no invented reviews, prices, or credentials.
3. It should feel like their brand, done well, not a template.
